Delete Your MatchBrain Account

MatchBrain · operated by Sajin Philip · Last updated: 7 June 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your MatchBrain account and the data associated with it. MatchBrain is a free football prediction app operated by Sajin Philip.

How to request account deletion

To delete your account, email us from the address associated with your MatchBrain account:

hello@getmatchbrain.com

Use the subject line "Delete my MatchBrain account". We will verify your request and permanently delete your account.

Steps:

  1. Send an email to hello@getmatchbrain.com from the email address you used to sign in to MatchBrain (your Google or Apple account email).
  2. Put "Delete my MatchBrain account" in the subject line.
  3. We will confirm your identity and process the deletion.

What data is deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:

  • Your profile (nickname and avatar)
  • Your account identifiers and the email associated with your account
  • All of your match predictions and tournament predictions
  • Your private league memberships and league points
  • Your leaderboard history

What data may be retained

DataRetention
Crash/diagnostic logs (Firebase Crashlytics)Retained on a rolling basis and not linked to your identity after account deletion

Timeframe

We action deletion requests promptly and complete account deletion within 30 days of verifying your request.
