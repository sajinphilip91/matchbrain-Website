Delete Your MatchBrain Account

MatchBrain · operated by Sajin Philip · Last updated: 9 June 2026

This page explains how to permanently delete your MatchBrain account and all associated data. MatchBrain is a free football prediction app operated by Sajin Philip.

Option 1 — Delete directly in the app

The fastest way to delete your account is directly inside the MatchBrain app. No email required.

  1. 1 Open the MatchBrain app and make sure you are signed in.
  2. 2 Tap the Profile tab (bottom navigation).
  3. 3 Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  4. 4 Confirm when prompted.

Your account and all associated data are permanently and immediately deleted. This action cannot be undone.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app or prefer to request deletion by email, send us a message from the address associated with your MatchBrain account:

Email us at:

hello@getmatchbrain.com

Use the subject line "Delete my MatchBrain account". We will verify your request and process the deletion within 30 days.

What data is deleted

When your account is deleted (by either method), we permanently remove:

  • Your profile — nickname and avatar
  • Your account identifiers and the email associated with your account
  • All of your match predictions and tournament predictions
  • Your private league memberships and points
  • Your leaderboard history
