3. The Game — How It Works

3.1 Match predictions

For each eligible match, you may submit predictions up to 24 hours before kick-off. Predictions lock automatically at kick-off and cannot be submitted or changed after the match has started. Each match presents five questions:

  1. Match result — which team wins, or a draw.
  2. First scorer — which team scores first.
  3. Total goals — the total number of goals scored, in a set bracket.
  4. Red card — whether any player receives a red card during the match.
  5. Both teams to score — whether both teams score at least one goal.

Predictions lock automatically at kick-off. You cannot submit or change predictions after the match has started.

3.2 Points

Each correct answer to a match prediction question earns 20 points, for a maximum of 100 points per match. Points are awarded automatically after matches are scored.

3.3 Tournament predictions

Before the tournament prediction lock date, you may also submit predictions for the overall tournament outcome, worth up to 500 points:

  • 100 pts — Tournament champion
  • 100 pts — Runner-up
  • 50 pts each — All four semi-finalists (×4 = 200 pts)
  • 50 pts — Tournament top scorer
  • 50 pts — Tournament's lowest-scoring team

Tournament predictions lock before the first knockout match kicks off and cannot be changed after that point.

3.4 Leaderboards and leagues

Registered users appear on the global leaderboard. You may also create or join private leagues with friends using a unique invite code. League scores are based on the same points system described above.
